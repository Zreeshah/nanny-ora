## Content quality rules

- Lead a section with a direct, self-contained answer only where it can be evidenced.
- Use original NannyOra information only when it is visible in the product or owner-confirmed.
- Link to primary New Zealand sources for employment, tax, Police vetting, emergency health and other sensitive claims.
- Keep profile discovery above long-form supporting content on commercial pages.
- Do not create pages for filter combinations, suburbs or specialist variations without distinct intent, real profile coverage and unique value.
