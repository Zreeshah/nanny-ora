## Recording rules

1. Run each prompt separately in ChatGPT Search, Perplexity and Google AI features where available.
2. Capture the answer date, result locale and exact cited URL. Save screenshots only where permitted and avoid recording family or nanny personal data.
3. Record an accuracy issue when an answer overstates local availability, treats all profiles as vetted, or gives legal/tax advice without caveat.
4. Do not infer citations from organic rank or a brand mention.

## Referral and conversion reporting

- In GA4, create a report for session source/medium and landing page, then inspect referral traffic from known AI referrers such as `chatgpt.com` and `perplexity.ai` when present.
- Track enquiries, family registrations and membership starts as separate downstream events. Referral attribution is incomplete and should not be treated as a census of AI-driven visits.
- In Search Console, use the dedicated generative-AI reporting view if it is available to this property; otherwise record the limitation and retain the normal query/page exports.
